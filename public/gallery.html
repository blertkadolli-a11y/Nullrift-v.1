FAQ

Questions, answered.

What do I actually get?

Each system comes as a detailed prompt and spec file. Copy it, paste it into your AI coding tool of choice, and you get the full production-ready section back — physics, interactions, accessibility and teardown included. Everything running in the gallery above was built from exactly these prompts.

What is the difference between an animation and a template?

An animation is one section — a hero, a reveal, a product picker — that you drop into a site you already have. A template is a complete page: layout, type, motion and responsive behaviour composed together, ready to become your whole site. Both come as a prompt, and every plan covers both.

What does “unlock 3 a month” mean?

On the $7 Monthly plan you can open up to 3 new systems per billing month, and anything you unlock stays unlocked for as long as you’re subscribed. The allowance refills on your renewal date. Cancel whenever you like.

Monthly, Pro or Studio — which should I pick?

Monthly ($7) suits the occasional build. Pro ($89.99, paid once) pays for itself fast if you ship regularly — the whole library plus every new system for a year. Studio ($899) is for when you don’t want to build anything at all: we get on a call and I make the entire website for you, animations included.

Is Pro really a one-time payment?

Yes. Pro and Team are paid once — there is nothing to cancel. You get the full library immediately and every system added over the following 12 months. After that year your existing systems stay yours permanently; you simply stop receiving new ones.

How does the Team plan work?

Team is Pro with six seats on one account, paid once. Use it across client work and internal projects — there is no per-project licence and no attribution required.

What actually happens on the Studio call?

We talk through what the site needs to be — the pages, the feel, the animations you want and the ones you don’t. You describe it however you like; sketches, a site you admire, or just talking. I go away and build it, come back with it working, and we adjust until it’s what you pictured. You keep all the code at the end.

What is the refund policy?

On Studio, if it isn’t what you wanted, tell me within the first 7 days and I refund 80% of what you paid, no argument. The remaining 20% covers the call and the work already started on your build.

Can I use these in client work?

Yes, on every tier. Commercial use is included and no attribution is required. The only thing you cannot do is resell the prompts themselves as a competing product.

Do I need a framework or a build step?

Not for the animations. Every one is plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript — no framework, no bundler, no npm install. Drop the module in, point it at an element, and it runs. Templates vary: Oblique is the same, while HADAL is a Vite + React Three Fiber site with a 3D model generated in Blender, so it needs Node and Blender installed. Your AI tool sets it all up from the prompt.
