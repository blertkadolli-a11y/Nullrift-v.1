Questions

About the templates.

What is in a template prompt?

The whole site, written down: every page, every line of copy, every colour, every animation and the technical decisions behind them, taken from the finished build you can preview above. Paste it into an AI coding tool that can create files and run commands, and it builds the site from start to finish.

What do I need installed?

Oblique is plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript — nothing to install. HADAL and WOBL are React Three Fiber sites whose 3D models are generated in Blender, so they need Node.js and Blender. AUTOCARS is a Next.js site and needs Node.js. Each prompt lists its requirements at the top, and your AI tool sets the project up.

How do I make it my own?

Build it as written, then tell your AI tool what to change: your name, your work, your copy, your colours. The templates are built around content slots, so swapping the fictional brand for yours is a matter of describing it.

How long does a build take?

Longer than an animation. Oblique is a single sitting. The 3D templates are full projects — models, scenes and several pages — and are best run in a tool and plan that allows a long session. The prompt is precise, but AI output is not identical every time, so expect a review round or two.

Do they work on phones?

Yes. Every template is responsive from 360px, works with touch, and handles reduced motion — it is part of what the prompt asks for, not an afterthought.

Are templates included in my plan?

Yes. Pro and Team include every template, plus every new one for 12 months. On Monthly a template counts as one of your 3 unlocks. Plans and billing are on the pricing page.
