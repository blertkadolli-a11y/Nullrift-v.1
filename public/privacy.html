Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

NULLRIFT sells prompts for web animations and website templates. This page explains what personal data we collect when you use nullrift.design, why, who processes it for us, and what you can ask us to do with it. Questions: nullriftdesign@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account details. Your email address, the username and profile picture you choose, and, if you set one, your password. Passwords are stored only as a salted hash by our authentication provider; nobody at NULLRIFT can see them.
  • Sign-in with Google, Apple or GitHub. If you use one of these, that provider shares your email address and, where available, your name and profile picture with us. We do not get your password for that service or access to anything else in it.
  • Purchases. Plans are sold through Polar (polar.sh), which acts as the merchant of record: it takes the payment, handles sales tax and sends your receipt. We receive a record of what you bought, your name and the email it was bought with. We never see or store your card details.
  • Emails you send us. If you contact us, we keep the conversation so we can reply and help.
  • Technical data. Like every website, our hosting provider records standard request logs (IP address, browser, pages requested, time) to run and secure the service.

What we store on your device

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. The site stores a few items in your browser’s local storage: your colour-theme choice, and, while you are signed in, your sign-in session. If you untick “Keep me signed in”, the session is kept only until you close the tab.

Why we use it

  • To create and secure your account and sign you in (performing our contract with you).
  • To give you the prompts included in your plan, and to handle purchases and refunds.
  • To send emails you need: sign-in codes, account confirmation and password resets. We do not send marketing email unless you ask for it.
  • To keep the service working and protect it from abuse (our legitimate interest).

Who processes it for us

  • Supabase — accounts, sign-in and profile data (stored in the EU, Ireland).
  • Vercel — website hosting and request logs.
  • Our email delivery provider — sends sign-in and password emails.
  • Polar — checkout, payments, tax and receipts, as merchant of record (see Polar’s privacy policy).
  • Google Fonts and cdnjs (Cloudflare) — deliver fonts and code libraries, so they receive your IP address when pages load.
  • Google, Apple, GitHub — only if you choose to sign in with them.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not share it with anyone else except where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your account data while your account is open. If you delete your account we remove your profile and sign-in data within 30 days, except purchase records we must keep for tax and accounting law.

Your rights

You can ask us to give you a copy of your data, correct it, delete it or your account, or stop using it for a particular purpose. Email nullriftdesign@gmail.com from the address on your account and we will reply within 30 days. If you are in the EU or UK you can also complain to your local data-protection authority.

Children

NULLRIFT is not meant for children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top. Significant changes will be announced on the site or by email.
